Inventory Settings

Select how the system calculates the cost of inventory issued. This setting will be used by the Stock Movement module.
Important accounting note: LIFO is included as an internal costing option for testing and management purposes. However, LIFO is not permitted under PFRS/IAS 2 for financial reporting. Use FIFO or Moving Weighted Average for PFRS-compliant inventory reporting.
