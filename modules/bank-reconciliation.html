Bank Reconciliation

This page is reserved for the bank reconciliation workflow.

Not yet enabled: The current Excel database does not contain a bank statement import or bank transaction ledger sheet. A proper reconciliation needs those records to compare statement lines against book transactions. This page intentionally does not create or modify accounting data.

Required database additions

  • BankTransactions (book-side deposits, withdrawals, charges, and transfers)
  • BankStatementLines (imported statement date, description, reference, debit, and credit)
  • Reconciliation records (matched items, outstanding items, statement balance, and book balance)

Once these sheets are added, this page can be connected to a real reconciliation process.
